The Morning Picture

Starlink for Indonesian schools and clinics

Musk amplified two Starlink stories from Indonesia: "Starlink saves lives," he posted at 11:36 a.m. EST Thursday, and "Starlink enables education in remote areas" at 4:32 a.m. EST Friday. Katingan Regency's local government is distributing Starlink kits to schools and clinics, according to official Diskominfostandi notices.

The same coverage window ends with Falcon 9 USSF-432 set for tonight from Vandenberg and SPCX's sharpest one-day drop since listing.

I · Human Spaceflight & Launches

USSF-432 is due tonight from Vandenberg

A four-hour window opens at 7:46 p.m. EST; Flight 15 slips one day to NET Nov. 13.

SpaceX is targeting Friday, Nov. 6, for a Falcon 9 launch of USSF-432 from Space Launch Complex 4E at Vandenberg Space Force Base, with a four-hour window opening at 7:46 p.m. EST and a backup on Saturday at 7:32 p.m. EST, SpaceX said. The Federal Aviation Administration's operations-plan advisory lists the same primary window as SpaceX R-4, 0046z–0529z Friday night (7:46 p.m.–12:29 a.m. EST), with a Saturday backup, on the ATCSCC advisory. Launch Library 2 lists the mission as Go for Launch; the booster is set to land on the droneship Of Course I Still Love You.

Flight 15 moves to NET Nov. 13

Starship Flight 15, the fourth flight of Starship V3 from Orbital Launch Pad 2 at Starbase, Texas, is now listed for no earlier than Friday, Nov. 13, on Launch Library 2 (day precision, status still to be determined). SpaceNotices reported Thursday that a CADENA presentation had delayed the flight by one day to NET Nov. 13; NASASpaceflight amplified the note. Thursday's Dispatch had listed Flight 15 only for November with no day. SpaceX still plans to try to catch both the ship and its Super Heavy booster.

Pad 39A water deluge

NASASpaceflight posted video Thursday of a full water-deluge test at the Starship pad under construction at Launch Complex 39A at Kennedy Space Center, Florida. USSF-627 follows Monday, Nov. 9, at 10:03 p.m. EST and USSF-317 on Thursday, Nov. 12, at 11:23 p.m. EST, both from SLC-4E, with matching FAA windows on the same advisory (Launch Library 2; ATCSCC).

Quick Hit

In-flight gaming. @Starlink quoted United Airlines Thursday evening on high-speed, low-latency gaming over Starlink in the cabin.

III · Starlink

Katingan kits for schools and clinics

Local officials in Central Kalimantan are handing out Starlink units; Musk amplified both the education and clinic stories.

Kabupaten Katingan's communications office said the regency received 287 Starlink kits in 2026 from the Central Kalimantan provincial government, with 174 allocated to education coordinators, early-childhood centers, elementary schools and junior highs, and 35 to community health centers (puskesmas) and auxiliary clinics, according to a Diskominfostandi notice dated Oct. 12. Symbolic handovers and activation training for schools took place that week; the clinic batch was handed over Oct. 8. Four villages remain blank-spot and about 30 have weak conventional signals, the office said.

"Starlink enables education in remote areas," Musk posted at 4:32 a.m. EST Friday, quoting a report on the Katingan school distribution. A day earlier, at 11:36 a.m. EST Thursday, he wrote "Starlink saves lives," quoting a report that Pangkep's local government had equipped Sailus Health Center — a clinic said to serve 10 remote islands — with Starlink so staff can send referrals online.

United shows cabin gaming

@Starlink quoted a United Airlines cabin video Thursday evening: "Game in-flight with Starlink's high-speed, low-latency internet." Free Standard-kit upgrades for early customers, confirmed Wednesday, remain in effect per Starlink's support page.

IV · Markets

SPCX falls 9% in a broad selloff

The stock closed at $123.62, underperforming even a weak Nasdaq 100, on volume 72% above its 30-session average.

SpaceX shares fell $12.30, or 9.05%, to $123.62 on Thursday, according to Yahoo Finance via yfinance. Volume hit 169.2 million shares, 72% above the 30-session average of 98.3 million. The Nasdaq 100 dropped 6.36% and the S&P 500 4.36% the same day; satellite peers Rocket Lab, AST SpaceMobile and Iridium also sold off. SPCX is now 8.4% below its $135 IPO price and 23.2% below its June 12 first close of $160.95.

China Renaissance's new Buy initiation at $194.20, dated Thursday morning on Yahoo Finance, still implies about 57% upside from the new close but sits well below the $257.11 mean target from 36 analysts.

Quick Hit

Weekly options. The Nov. 6 SPCX weekly expires today; ATM implied volatility on that expiry was 116.8% in the CBOE snapshot at Thursday's close.
