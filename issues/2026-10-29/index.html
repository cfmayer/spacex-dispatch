The Morning Picture

Musk: Ship 42 will be the first ship caught by the tower

"Ship 42 static fires complete," SpaceX posted at 12:10 p.m. EDT Wednesday. "The Starship preparing for Flight 15 performed a full duration, six-engine static fire, and fired a single Raptor engine to simulate a deorbit burn." Quoting the post at 10:37 p.m., Elon Musk wrote: "As the prophecy foretold, Starship 42 will be the first tower catch."

Ship 42 left the Massey's test site that night and was moved along Highway 4 back to the production site, Tracking The Starbase posted at 11:15 p.m., and was "back in the barn" by 2:01 a.m. Thursday (post). SpaceX has not announced a date for Flight 15, which Launch Library 2 lists for no earlier than November from Pad 2 at Starbase.

I · Human Spaceflight & Launches

Starfall delivers Air Force cargo from orbit

AFRL recovered its cargo pods after the capsule, launched on Bandwagon-5, splashed down in the Pacific.

The Air Force Research Laboratory recovered cargo pods that flew to orbit and back inside SpaceX's Starfall capsule, which launched on the Bandwagon-5 Falcon 9 mission from Cape Canaveral on October 20 and splashed down in the Pacific Ocean, Space.com reported. "With support from SpaceX, the AFRL agile cargo pods were recovered after splashdown in the Pacific Ocean," AFRL said in an October 26 post.

"This was a critical achievement to demonstrate the potential of Rocket Cargo delivery for point-to-point rapid global mobility for the Future Force," said AFRL's Adam Remaly. The pods carried temperature, shock and vibration sensors along with medical equipment, ration packs, a satellite-linked tracker, batteries, a reverse-osmosis desalinator and survival gear, and AFRL fit-checked a life raft in the capsule (Space.com).

Starfall, which debuted in June, is about 3.1 meters (10.2 feet) across and can return up to 1,000 kilograms (2,200 pounds), with a carbon-fiber heat shield, cold-gas thrusters and parachutes, according to an FAA environmental assessment cited by Space.com. AFRL's separate plan to test Starship cargo landings on Johnston Atoll was suspended in July 2025. The Verge also covered the recovery.

"No need to paint stainless"

Replying to a Dwarkesh Podcast clip on Starship's stainless-steel construction, Musk wrote at 2:20 a.m. EDT Thursday: "Also, no need to paint stainless. Paint adds more mass & cost."

III · Starlink

Starlink keeps phones working after Spain's train crash

Movistar and Orange customers near the collision site can text and use apps through satellites while towers are down.

"Starlink Mobile is providing free connectivity to @orange_es and @Telefonica customers affected by the devastating train collision in Spain," Starlink posted at 7:37 p.m. EDT Wednesday. "Families, communities, and businesses with compatible LTE phones can stay connected via apps and texts even when cell towers are down."

The collision on Wednesday between a Renfe AVE and an Iryo train on the Madrid–Seville high-speed line near Urda, in the province of Toledo, Movistar and Orange switched on Starlink service in the area under an authorization from Telecom Castilla-La Mancha to use its frequencies, ADSLZone reported. The carriers did the same during this summer's wildfires near Madrid. El Español also reported the activation.

"We're supporting communities in Spain with @Starlink Mobile during this difficult time," SpaceX's Stephanie Bednarek posted at 9:37 p.m. Musk replied: "Standard practice."

Pentagon watchdog declines Ukraine review

The Defense Department's inspector general, Platte Moring, declined a request from nine senators of both parties, led by Thom Tillis and Jeanne Shaheen, to investigate slowdowns of Starshield and Starlink service in Ukraine, saying the Air Force and Space Force were better placed to review it, CNN reported Wednesday. Speeds were fully restored on Saturday, according to the report.

IV · AI & Compute

A referral program for Grok Bot

Invited users get a two-week Premium trial; Musk says he has Grok Bot shop around for lower prices.

SpaceX introduced a referral program for Grok Bot under which an invited friend gets a two-week free trial of Premium and the referrer gets a free usage reset, valid for six months, Sawyer Merritt posted at 3:50 p.m. EDT Wednesday.

"Amazon locked out my Grok Bot. So it just bought the same thing from a different site (at a lower price)," James Douma posted at 10:00 p.m. "That's what I do too. Just have @Bot buy it somewhere else at a lower price," Musk replied at 10:30 p.m. When Shopify's Tobi Lütke replied "Just in time for shop pay support," Musk quoted him: "Buy from @Shopify!"
