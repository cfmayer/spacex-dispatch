The Morning Picture

A Starshield speed limit hits Ukraine's front line; Musk says Washington decides

Ukrainian officials said military Starshield terminals were restricted while moving overnight into Wednesday, slowing drones and ground robots. "Starshield systems are owned and controlled by the US government. Any limits on speed or usage are decided by the US military and ultimately POTUS," Elon Musk replied to Ukraine's Mykhailo Fedorov, who had said connectivity at up to 160 km/h was being restored.

Cargo Dragon is due to dock at the International Space Station (ISS) at about 11 a.m. EDT today, and a Falcon 9 is scheduled to launch the USSF-481 mission from California at 7:44 p.m. EDT. SPCX fell 2.80% to $152.16 as a hot inflation report hit AI-linked stocks. There were no new SEC filings.

I · Human Spaceflight & Launches

Dragon arrives this morning; USSF-481 flies tonight

No SpaceX launches since CRS-35 on Tuesday; two events today.

CRS-35 docking, about 11 a.m.

Dragon is set to dock autonomously at the space-facing port of the Harmony module at about 11 a.m. EDT, with NASA coverage from 9:15 a.m. EDT, NASA said. It carries more than 6,300 pounds of supplies and research, including the last pair of ISS Roll-Out Solar Arrays in its trunk (NASA).

USSF-481, 7:44 p.m.

Falcon 9 is scheduled to launch the classified USSF-481 mission for the US Space Force, also called TH-2, from Space Launch Complex 4 East at Vandenberg Space Force Base, with the window opening at 7:44 p.m. EDT, Spaceflight Now lists. Booster B1081, on its 28th flight, will target the drone ship Of Course I Still Love You. The Federal Aviation Administration's (FAA) operations plan shows a primary window from 7:44 p.m. to 12:27 a.m. EDT and a backup on Friday.

III · Starlink

"Usage of Starlink is limited to civilian or defensive activities"

Ukrainian units said terminals on fast-moving drones and vehicles were slowed without notice; Poland's foreign minister weighed in.

"On the night of October 14, without prior notice to the Defense Forces, SpaceX unilaterally restricted the operation of Starshield terminals while in motion," a Ukrainian defense ministry official told reporters in Kyiv, Politico reported; problems occurred at speeds from 72 to 161 km/h depending on the vehicle and unit. Ukrainian drones lost some of their speed, AP reported. The decision was not a high-level US policy decision, two US officials told CNN.

Musk wrote that Starlink "is a commercial service owned and controlled by SpaceX," limited "to civilian or defensive activities." "Hey, @elonmusk, big man, are you playing politics with your products again? As your European customers, we are watching you," Polish Foreign Minister Radosław Sikorski posted; Poland funds more than 29,000 Starlink terminals in Ukraine, Interfax-Ukraine reported. Late Wednesday Musk posted "Time for Zelensky to go."

Quick Hit

Curaçao. Starlink service is now available in Curaçao, Musk posted on Wednesday.

IV · AI & Compute

Grok Voice 3.0, and Amazon shuts out Grok Bot

SpaceXAI released Grok Voice 3.0, which it called "the world's most intelligent speech-to-speech model," saying it "responds in under a second, and costs less than other frontier models"; Musk quoted the announcement. It debuted first on the Artificial Analysis Speech to Speech Index with a score of 86.9, according to a post Musk shared.

A user posted that Amazon had blocked a Grok Bot from ordering, citing "unauthorized AI agent" access. "Just ask Grok @Bot to buy what you want someplace else and get a better deal," Musk replied. Separately he wrote: "If Grok @Bot makes a mistake with a purchase or leaks your card (never happened yet), SpaceX will make you whole." SpaceXAI testifies before the New York City Council's Committee on Technology tomorrow at 10 a.m. EDT (amNY).

V · Filings: SEC, FAA & FCC

Starbase scoping is open; nothing new at the SEC

Wednesday's FAA notice on up to 456 Starship launches a year opened a comment period that runs to November 30.

FAA

  • Notice of intent · Oct 14 — The FAA will prepare a Programmatic Environmental Impact Statement (PEIS) on SpaceX's proposal to increase Starship-Super Heavy operations at the Boca Chica launch site at Starbase and to add Starship landing and expenditure locations (docket FAA-2026-12808; 91 FR 65289). Later license changes and temporary airspace closures fall within its scope. Federal Register
  • What is proposed — Phase 1 allows up to 100 launches and 200 landings a year, at any time on any day; Phase 2, expected within several years, raises the cap to 456 launches and 912 landings, with launches on weekdays only, Invezz reported. The current limit is 25 launches and 50 landings. Four in-person scoping meetings are set for November 17 and 19 and a virtual one for November 18; comments are due November 30, SpacePolicyOnline reported.
  • Notice of intent · Sep 29 — Scoping comments on Starship at Space Launch Complex 37, Cape Canaveral (up to 76 launches a year), closed Wednesday (docket FAA-2026-10792). Federal Register
