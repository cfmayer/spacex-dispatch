The Morning Picture

Free Standard kits for early Starlink customers

"Free new dish for all early Starlink customers," Musk posted at 10:02 a.m. EST Wednesday, quoting a report that Starlink is rolling out free upgrades for its earliest users still on first-generation kits. Eligible customers "can receive a new Standard kit at no cost," with a free mount and optional free installation, according to Starlink support language quoted by Sawyer Merritt and confirmed by Musk.

The confirmation came the same day SpaceX and Qualcomm detailed Dragonwing 64T64R radios for Starlink Mobile's terrestrial 5G build, and a day before Falcon 9 is due to loft USSF-432 from Vandenberg.

I · Human Spaceflight & Launches

USSF-432 is set for Friday evening

A four-hour Vandenberg window opens at 7:46 p.m. EST; Flight 15 remains listed only for November.

SpaceX is targeting Friday, Nov. 6, for a Falcon 9 launch of USSF-432 from Space Launch Complex 4E at Vandenberg Space Force Base, with a four-hour window opening at 7:46 p.m. EST and a backup on Saturday at 7:32 p.m. EST, SpaceX said. The booster will fly for the eighth time and land on the droneship Of Course I Still Love You. Launch Library 2 lists the mission as Go for Launch.

Flight 15 still has no day

Starship Flight 15, the fourth flight of Starship V3 from Orbital Launch Pad 2 at Starbase, Texas, remains listed only for November without a specific day on Launch Library 2. SpaceX plans to try to catch both the ship and its Super Heavy booster, a major reusability test. USSF-627 follows on Monday, Nov. 9, at 10:03 p.m. EST and USSF-317 on Thursday, Nov. 12, at 11:23 p.m. EST, both from SLC-4E (Launch Library 2).

III · Starlink

Free kits, and 64T64R for Starlink Mobile

Musk confirmed free Standard upgrades for early customers; Qualcomm will supply Dragonwing radios for the 2027 tower build.

"Eligible customers who were among the first to connect with Starlink, and who are still using a first-generation kit can receive a new Standard kit at no cost. Customers will be provided with a free mount, and they can opt in for free installation. If you are eligible for this upgrade, you will receive an invitation by email," Starlink support language said, as quoted by Sawyer Merritt and confirmed by Musk at 10:02 a.m. EST Wednesday. More detail is on Starlink's support page.

Dragonwing radios for tens of thousands of sites

SpaceX selected Qualcomm's Dragonwing QRU100 and QDU100 platforms, plus commercial-grade Layer 1 software, for Starlink Mobile's U.S. terrestrial 5G base stations, Qualcomm said Wednesday. The radios use 64T64R Massive MIMO, Converge Digest reported; rollout is planned to begin in 2027 under a multiyear agreement covering tens of thousands of sites. "Starlink Mobile is building the first fully-integrated US nationwide satellite and terrestrial network," @Starlink posted at 8:32 a.m. EST Wednesday, adding that its 5G base stations "will leverage @Qualcomm chips to accelerate deployment, starting in 2027."

Financial terms were not disclosed. Starlink accounted for 36% of SpaceX's third-quarter revenue and 85% of its operating profit, CNBC reported. SpaceX has not named tower partners.

IV · Markets & Analysts

China Renaissance starts at Buy, $194.20

A new initiation sits well below the Street's mean; SPCX closed Wednesday at $135.92.

China Renaissance initiated coverage of SpaceX with a Buy rating and a $194.20 price target, according to Yahoo Finance rating data dated Thursday morning. That target implies about 43% upside from Wednesday's close of $135.92, but sits below the $257.11 mean target from 36 analysts. Bank of America, Pivotal Research and others raised targets into the mid-$200s after the Oct. 21–22 earnings window.

SPCX fell 3.81% on Wednesday, giving back Tuesday's 3.62% gain, as volume ran 41% above its 30-session average. The Nasdaq 100 rose 0.91% the same day.
