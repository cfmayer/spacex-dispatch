The Morning Picture

Revenue up 161% as AI becomes the biggest business

SpaceX reported third-quarter revenue of $14.58 billion, against the $12.72 billion analysts expected, and net income of $4.4 billion, compared with a $1.1 billion loss a year earlier, Reuters reported. The Super Intelligence (SI) segment brought in $7.6 billion, more than Starlink and launch combined, according to the company's results release.

"We are in an inflection moment for SpaceX across launch, connectivity and superintelligence," Musk said on the call. SPCX, which closed down 0.11% at $147.79 on Tuesday, rose 4.6% after hours, Bloomberg Law reported. That evening, a Starlink launch from California was scrubbed for a technical issue.

Thursday · Lock-up release

Up to 1.3 billion more shares can trade

Thu Oct 22 · second full trading day after the results

Under the prospectus, up to 1.3 billion locked-up Class A shares, 28% of those under the 180-day lock-up, become sellable on Thursday, followed by up to 328.4 million (7%) on Saturday, October 24, and the rest on December 8. Musk's shares stay locked until the 366th day after the offering.

I · Third-Quarter Results

A profit, and a Starship catch attempt next month

Capital spending nearly tripled to $19.6 billion, most of it for AI compute.

The numbers

Operating income was $2.79 billion and adjusted EBITDA (earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization) $8.81 billion; diluted GAAP earnings were 35 cents a share, according to the release. On the IBES basis SpaceX earned 45 cents, against a 13-cent average estimate, Reuters reported. SI revenue rose 830% from a year earlier, driven by new cloud-services agreements, the Cursor acquisition, which contributed $608 million, and Grok adoption; SI operating income was $431 million after $1.2 billion of Cursor-related share-based compensation.

SpaceX makes its AI money mainly by renting data-center capacity to developers including Anthropic, Google and Reflection AI, and AI took $16.4 billion of the quarter's $19.6 billion in capital spending, a level the company expects to hold near term, Reuters noted. Nameplate compute reached 1.7 gigawatts (GW) on September 30. Cash and equivalents were $51.3 billion, plus $38.6 billion of marketable securities (release).

Space: 35 launches

Space revenue was $1.8 billion, up 84% from the second quarter, on 12 customer and 23 internal launches, among them NASA's Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope, and Starship Flight 14 delivered 26 Starlink V3 satellites, about 10 times the downlink capacity a Falcon 9 Starlink launch adds. New orders include three NASA crew missions to the International Space Station, 18 National Security Space Launch missions, three commercial Starfall missions and a Starship payload to the lunar surface (release).

"For Flight 15, which we expect to happen next month, we're going to attempt to catch both the booster and the ship," Musk said on the call. "Success is not guaranteed on these first flights, but I'd say it's more likely than not that this succeeds next flight." He expects weekly Starship flights at some point in the first half of next year and twice-weekly or more by the end of next year, according to a Motley Fool transcript; Spaceflight Now posted the same targets at 4:42 p.m. EDT.

Guidance

Chief Financial Officer Bret Johnsen guided to fourth-quarter capital spending of $35 billion to $40 billion and first-quarter 2027 revenue of $33 billion to $37 billion, and said SpaceX remains on track for $100 billion in annual recurring revenue (ARR) by the end of this year. Backlog was $63 billion on September 30, about $42 billion of it expected to be recognized within a year. Nameplate compute should reach 2.5 to 3 gigawatts by year-end; "I would be surprised if we brought on less than 13 gigawatts of compute by the end of next year," Musk said, putting ARR at the end of 2027 at "somewhere between $500 billion and $1 trillion" (transcript).

Quick Hit

Starbase, Louisiana. SpaceX announced plans for a second Starship spaceport in Louisiana, "designed to support thousands of Starship flights a year, with the first launch targeted for 2029," according to the release.

The merger question

Musk said Tesla and SpaceX have each formed special committees of independent directors to study a combination at shareholders' request. "I do think there's a lot of merit to combining the companies," he said, but "some people out there assume that a SpaceX and Tesla merger is a certainty. I think that would be overstating it," Bloomberg Law reported. Tesla shares rose 1.2% after hours.

Starship Flight 15

Pad 2 at Starbase "is ready for Booster 22," after teams ran a full test of its water deluge system Tuesday evening, a post shared by NASASpaceflight showed, and a second test followed about 10 minutes later. Booster 22's move to the pad should be next, while Ship 43 now looks likely to go to the Massey's test site rather than the launch tower, NASASpaceflight wrote at 6:52 p.m. EDT.

Flight 15 is targeting November 8, according to NASASpaceflight, with Ship 42 and Booster 22; Flight 16, likely Booster 23 and Ship 43, is aimed at December and could be the first Starship launch from Launch Complex 39A in Florida. "We expect the license for our Flight 15 to include return to launch site and tower catch for both stages," Shotwell said on the call (transcript).

III · Starlink

13.6 million subscribers and a path to "several hundred billion"

Subscriber counts beat one estimate and missed another, and revenue per user is below 2023 levels.

Starlink ended September with 13.6 million consumer subscribers, up from 12.0 million three months earlier. That topped the 12.19 million analysts expected, Reuters reported, but fell short of the 14.2 million Bloomberg analysts expected, Business Insider reported; the count excludes enterprise and aviation customers. Average monthly revenue per consumer subscriber was $69, down from $99 in 2023 as cheaper plans roll out abroad, though up 5% from the second quarter. Connectivity revenue rose 69% from a year earlier to $5.2 billion, with enterprise and government revenue up 91% (release).

About 3,800 aircraft had been fitted with Starlink by the end of September. "Aviation remains a major growth opportunity, as we're still early in the installation phase with many of the largest carriers worldwide," Chief Financial Officer Bret Johnsen said on the call, Business Insider reported.

Starlink Mobile

"The vast majority of mobile connectivity is actually WiFi," President Gwynne Shotwell said. "As far as the mix goes, I think we expect that the S-band spectrum will provide the majority. And then obviously, you need the low-band spectrum, the 800 megahertz spectrum, to basically penetrate buildings," Business Insider reported. The 800 MHz purchase, agreed October 8, is expected to close in the first half of 2027 (release).

"I can say we do see a clear path to several hundred billion dollars a year in connectivity revenue," Musk said, adding that the plan deserved "a forum that's not limited to a few minutes at an earnings call," The Verge reported. Starlink Mobile passed 22 million monthly users, up 37% from the second quarter; SpaceX plans to fly its second-generation Mobile satellites through next year and start U.S. service with the new spectrum in early 2028, Shotwell said. Users will need "a few hundred" V3 satellites in orbit to feel faster service, which she expects by the middle of next year (transcript).

IV · AI & Compute

"We could rent out our compute several times over"

Musk said SpaceX is "hounded relentlessly by every hyperscaler" for capacity and prefers short contracts at spot prices.

Roughly half of SpaceX's compute is meant for its own use and half for rent, a split that could move "60-40 one way or the other," Musk said. "Our belief is that the value of compute per watt will increase over time, not decrease," he said, adding that short-term contracts are renewing "at higher dollars per watt, not lower." Johnsen said new deployments pay back in less than a year at current pricing, which is tracking slightly above the high end of the $30 to $50 per watt range discussed last quarter (transcript).

Paid weekly users of Grok Bot, the company's agent launched in August, reached 800,000 earlier this month, and Grok Enterprise customers with more than $1 million in run-rate revenue rose 50% from the second quarter, Shotwell said. SpaceX is casting turbine blades and vanes in its rocket-engine foundry, which Musk said lets it bring power online "12 to 18 months faster than anyone else" (transcript).

Quick Hit

Little League. Musk said SpaceX launches almost 90% of all mass to orbit, against about 6% for China, and has put the same team on building data centers: "This is a bit like the New York Yankees playing in Little League. It's not fair" (transcript).
