The Morning Picture

Two Falcon 9s in ten hours: Starlink from California, then cargo Dragon to the station

Falcon 9 launched 27 Starlink satellites from Vandenberg at 8:28 p.m. EDT Monday, the first Falcon 9 Starlink mission since September 19, and then sent NASA's 35th cargo Dragon (CRS-35) toward the International Space Station (ISS) from Cape Canaveral at 6:33 a.m. EDT today. Dragon is due to dock on Thursday at about 11 a.m. EDT; its booster landed at Landing Zone 40. A Bandwagon-5 rideshare is now dated for Monday, October 19.

SpaceX's AI arm unified Grok, Cursor and X Premium into one five-tier plan, The X Subscription. Poland said two Turkish citizens were arrested in Germany over a fire at a Starlink ground station that Warsaw blames on Russian intelligence. India's communications ministry called complaints about Starlink's wait "baseless." SPCX rose 0.52% to $163.42. There were no new SEC or FAA filings.

I · Human Spaceflight & Launches

CRS-35 is on its way with the station's last new solar arrays

A predawn launch from Florida followed an evening Starlink flight from California, ending a three-week gap in Falcon 9 Starlink launches.

CRS-35: liftoff at 6:33 a.m.

Falcon 9 lifted off from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral Space Force Station at 6:33 a.m. EDT on Tuesday, SpaceX posted, and booster B1096, on its eighth flight, landed on Landing Zone 40. SpaceX then confirmed Dragon's separation and set autonomous docking for Thursday, October 15, at about 11 a.m. EDT.

Cargo Dragon C211, on its fourth flight, carries more than 6,300 pounds of supplies and research, NASA said. In its unpressurized trunk is the final pair of ISS Roll-Out Solar Arrays (iROSAs), and inside is a clamshell container to bring home the failed Canadarm2 joint removed during a June 30 spacewalk, Spaceflight Now reported. Experiments include making artificial retinas, growing 3D heart-tissue models and studying how inflammation is linked to neurodegenerative disease. Dragon will dock to the space-facing port of the Harmony module and stay about a month.

Starlink 15-25 flies

After four date changes, Starlink 15-25 lifted off from Space Launch Complex 4 East at Vandenberg Space Force Base at 8:28 p.m. EDT Monday, Spaceflight Now reported. Booster B1088 landed on the drone ship Of Course I Still Love You on its 20th flight, and SpaceX confirmed deployment of all 27 V2 Mini satellites at 9:30 p.m. EDT. It was the first Falcon 9 Starlink launch since September 19, the year's 112th Falcon 9 flight and its 82nd for Starlink, Space.com reported.

Starship: after the catch, a reflight

Musk's X Takeover remarks went beyond Flight 15's planned ship catch. "The next big milestone would be to refly both the booster and the ship," he said, describing a Flight 16 on which "all parts of an orbital rocket are reflown," Teslarati reported. He put Flight 15 "probably" a little before Thanksgiving. SpaceX has not published a date, and Launch Library 2 lists Flight 15 only for the fourth quarter.

III · Starlink

Arrests in the Polish ground-station fire; India says delays are not discrimination

Warsaw says the September fire was sabotage ordered by Russian intelligence. New Delhi says all three satellite licensees are at the same stage.

Poland

German police detained two Turkish citizens on behalf of Polish authorities over a September fire at a Starlink station in the Masovia region that burned its power system and backup generator, Al Jazeera reported on Monday. Interior Minister Marcin Kierwiński said they will face sabotage charges in Poland. "We have no doubt that Russian services are behind this act of sabotage," said Special Services Minister Tomasz Siemoniak. Officials said the attack aimed to disrupt internet service, including for Ukraine's military.

India

India's Ministry of Communications said security assessments are under way for all three licensed satellite operators, Starlink, Reliance's Jio and Eutelsat OneWeb, which are at broadly the same stage, and called claims that the framework is unfair "baseless and misconceived," DW reported. Reliance Jio chairman Akash Ambani declined to comment on Musk's posts: "We want to keep our heads down and deliver value to our Indian consumers."

IV · AI & Compute

One subscription for Grok, Cursor and X

"We've unified plans across Grok, Cursor, and X Premium," SpaceX Super Intelligence posted at 7:08 p.m. EDT Monday, introducing The X Subscription. It has five plans "from Free to Ultra," and each gives "more usage-per-dollar of our own models than the comparable plan it replaces," the company said. Paid plans are $8, $30, $100 and $200 a month, according to the pricing documentation. Current subscribers can keep their plans or switch and link their accounts; the rollout is gradual, SpaceXSI said.
