The Morning Picture

Starfall flies again on results day

A Falcon 9 lifted off from Cape Canaveral at 7:04 a.m. EDT Tuesday with Bandwagon-5, a rideshare of 32 payloads that includes the second flight of SpaceX's Starfall return capsule, a day late because of recovery weather. The booster landed on the drone ship A Shortfall of Gravitas, and payload deployments began about an hour after launch (SpaceX).

After the close, SpaceX reports its third quarter, its second report as a public company. Musk's pledged share for every American child will go through Trump Accounts, President Trump said Monday, The Verge reported. SPCX rose 1.32% to $147.96 on Monday.

Today · Q3 earnings

Third-quarter results after the close

Tue Oct 20 · results after the close · audio-only webcast 4:30 p.m. EDT

The webcast will also stream on X (SpaceX). Bloomberg's consensus as reported by Yahoo Finance calls for $12.7 billion in revenue, up from $7.8 billion in the second quarter, adjusted earnings of 15 cents a share and adjusted EBITDA (earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization) of $6.59 billion; the Connectivity segment, which includes Starlink, is expected to earn $1.66 billion on $4.45 billion in revenue, after second-quarter Connectivity revenue rose 66% to $4.3 billion, Reuters noted. LSEG data show $12.74 billion and 14 cents, according to Reuters, and Yahoo Finance's own tally is 16 cents on $13.00 billion. Investors are watching AI spending, which reached $15.8 billion in the second quarter, and new annual recurring revenue (ARR) figures; last month Chief Financial Officer Bret Johnsen indicated SpaceX would reach $100 billion in ARR by year-end. Under the prospectus, up to 1.3 billion more locked-up Class A shares, 28% of those under the 180-day lock-up, become sellable on the second full trading day after the results: Thursday, October 22.

I · Launches

Bandwagon-5 carries a second Starfall

The capsule is meant to bring experiments and products made in orbit back to Earth.

"Starfall enables affordable, routine access to a microgravity environment, providing unique opportunities for on-orbit manufacturing, scientific research, and testing," SpaceX posted minutes before launch. The capsule debuted on June 23; this time it rides with 31 customer payloads, among them Varda Space Industries' W-Series 7 capsule and Planet's Pelican-12 imaging satellite, and is the last to separate, about 2 hours 48 minutes after liftoff, Space.com reported. The FAA's environmental assessment describes Starfall as about 3.1 meters wide and 2,100 kilograms, able to return up to 1,000 kilograms.

The first stage, on its 19th flight, landed about nine minutes after launch. The launch had been postponed from Monday for poor weather in the recovery zone, Spaceflight Now lists, and on Tuesday the launch time moved from 6:30 to 7:04 a.m. EDT (Spaceflight Now). SpaceNews also covered the launch.

Starship Flights 15 and 16

Flight 15 is targeting November 8, with the goal of catching both Booster 22 and Ship 42; Ship 42's engines were removed for an extra cryogenic test, and Booster 22 is waiting to roll to Pad 2 for a static fire, NASASpaceflight reported. Musk has said the flight should come before Thanksgiving. Flight 16, likely Booster 23 and Ship 43, is aimed at December and could be the first Starship launch from Launch Complex 39A in Florida. Morgan Stanley's Adam Jonas thinks a catch of the ship on one of the two flights is possible, Yahoo Finance noted.

III · Starlink

Europe's big four carriers team up on satellite spectrum

Deutsche Telekom, Orange, Telefónica and Vodafone may bid together for direct-to-device airwaves.

The four operators signed a letter of intent on Monday to explore a joint bid for 2 GHz mobile satellite service (MSS) spectrum, which the European Commission is preparing to reassign, with the satellites to come from "a satellite partner still to be selected," Via Satellite reported. The letter does not replace existing partnerships; Deutsche Telekom was the first partner signed for SpaceX's second-generation Starlink Mobile service, and SpaceX now owns EchoStar spectrum that holds one of the current European MSS licenses, Via Satellite noted.

"Europe's operators want to avoid becoming effectively MVNOs [mobile virtual network operators] for satellite coverage, selling somebody else's service on somebody else's terms," Ookla analyst Luke Kehoe told Via Satellite. "Starlink's push into the U.S. mobile market makes that concern harder to ignore." Bloomberg Law framed the group as a way to fend off SpaceX.

The move mirrors AT&T, T-Mobile and Verizon, which formed a joint venture for direct-to-device service earlier this month and called for competition among satellite providers, Via Satellite noted. Vodafone is a longtime partner and investor in AST SpaceMobile, which said it "welcomes" the letter. "The sovereignty claim will also be tested in the satellite contract itself," Kehoe said. "European spectrum rights and ground infrastructure only go so far when a separate partner controls the constellation."

Quick Hit

Eleven million. "In the last year, Starlink Mobile has connected more than 11 million people across @VEONGroup 3 key markets: Ukraine, Kazakhstan, and Bangladesh," Starlink posted at 9:09 p.m. EDT Monday.

IV · Shareholders

The share gift goes through Trump Accounts

Seventy-two million shares would be worth about $10.5 billion at Friday's price.

President Trump announced Monday afternoon that Musk's SpaceX shares for children will be given through Trump Accounts, the tax-advantaged accounts for kids, after Musk thanked Brad Gerstner for "helping enable this," The Verge reported. The White House has said over 70 million accounts were created after some 60 million children were enrolled automatically; Michael and Susan Dell, Ray and Barbara Dalio, Gerstner and SpaceX President Gwynne Shotwell have also promised contributions.

"Yes, giving shares, which are stakes in the future economy, directly to the people is the way," Musk replied to a follower at 11:58 a.m. EDT Monday; his thanks to Gerstner came in a separate reply. Asked overnight that the accounts' default investment option no longer be State Street, he answered "Noted."

The unlock calendar

Thursday's release of up to 1.3 billion Class A shares, 28% of those under the 180-day lock-up, is followed by up to 328.4 million (7%) on Saturday, October 24, and the rest on December 8: up to 328.4 million shares, or 797.6 million if the "Additional Release Shares" were not freed after the first earnings report, according to the prospectus. A separate group of about 7.8 billion shares, about 60% of those outstanding after the offering and including all of Musk's, is locked longer: up to 351.9 million unlock after the fourth-quarter report, and Musk's shares are held until the 366th day after the offering with no early release.

V · AI & Compute

The question hanging over the results: financing

SpaceXSI lost $1.26 billion in the second quarter as AI spending doubled.

The AI unit, now called SpaceXSI, posted a second-quarter operating loss of $1.26 billion, against estimates of $2.39 billion, while AI spending rose to $15.8 billion from $7.7 billion in the first quarter, Yahoo Finance reported. "Need for additional financing remains a key overhang for sentiment, especially given the recent neocloud debt saga," Morgan Stanley's Adam Jonas wrote Monday; he models debt issuance of $60 billion, $80 billion and $90 billion in 2027, 2028 and 2029.

Evercore ISI expects SpaceX to exit 2026 at an annualized revenue run-rate of about $104 billion, with the AI segment doing "most of the heavy lifting," and estimates capital spending of about $151 billion in 2027, Reuters reported. "In terms of metrics, it would have to be Starlink revenue growth more than launch counts," said Shawn Campbell, an adviser at Camelthorn Investments who owns shares in SpaceX and Tesla.

Quick Hit

Faster feeds. Musk announced two changes to X's timeline recommendations Monday night: a new ranking model that better predicts time spent and a retrieval model trained to surface posts people read to the end. Rewriting the X Android app "was also a big deal," he added.

Musk also promoted Grok Bot, the company's AI agent, writing that it "might turn out to be your favorite employee" (post), and answered John Carmack: "Grok's engineering analysis is good" (post).
