The Morning Picture

Morgan Stanley nearly doubles its 2027 forecast

Morgan Stanley analyst Adam Jonas now expects SpaceX to generate $175 billion in revenue in 2027 and $289 billion in 2028, increases of 93% and 84% from his previous forecasts, Investing.com reported Wednesday. His 2028 operating income estimate rose 72% to $119 billion, and the bank now sees SpaceX turning free-cash-flow positive in 2031 instead of 2035, with cumulative external borrowing of about $390 billion rather than $670 billion.

At roughly $140 a share and a $1.91 trillion market value, SpaceX trades at 17.6 times projected 2028 adjusted earnings, Jonas calculates, slightly below the bank's 17.7 times for the S&P 500. His $300 target implies about 114% upside. In his model the space and connectivity businesses account for about $123 of the share price, leaving $17 for AI (Investing.com). Jonas expects earnings to grow about 150% a year from 2026 to 2030. The shares still fell about 5% early Wednesday after the midterm elections.

I · Human Spaceflight & Launches

Flight 15 is set to try catching both stages

Morgan Stanley calls the next Starship flight a catalyst; three Space Force missions line up at Vandenberg.

The next Starship flight, expected this month, is a potentially important catalyst, Jonas wrote, as quoted by Investing.com. SpaceX plans to try to catch both the ship and its Super Heavy booster after launch, a major test of its plan to make the system fully reusable. Musk has said the attempt is more likely than not to succeed, according to Jonas.

Launch Library 2 lists Flight 15, the fourth flight of Starship V3, from Orbital Launch Pad 2 at Starbase, Texas, for November without a specific day. Tuesday's issue carried an unofficial target of no earlier than Nov. 12.

USSF-432 on Friday evening

SpaceX is targeting Friday, Nov. 6, for a Falcon 9 launch of USSF-432 from Space Launch Complex 4E at Vandenberg Space Force Base in California, with a four-hour window opening at 7:46 p.m. EST and a backup on Saturday at 7:32 p.m. EST, SpaceX said. The booster will fly for the eighth time and land on the droneship Of Course I Still Love You.

Two more Space Force missions get names

The Vandenberg windows listed as "R-5" and "R-6" now carry mission names: USSF-627 on Monday, Nov. 9, at 10:03 p.m. EST and USSF-317 on Thursday, Nov. 12, at 11:23 p.m. EST, both on Falcon 9 from Space Launch Complex 4E, according to Launch Library 2, which describes each as a classified mission for the U.S. Space Force.

III · Starlink

Qualcomm chips for Starlink Mobile's towers

A multiyear deal covers base stations for tens of thousands of new cell sites, with rollout starting next year.

SpaceX struck a multiyear deal with Qualcomm to supply Dragonwing cellular infrastructure hardware and software for the base stations of Starlink Mobile, its U.S. carrier, CNBC reported Wednesday. Qualcomm called it its largest 5G initiative to date. Financial terms were not disclosed, and the terrestrial network is meant to "complement Starlink's satellite capabilities," with rollout to begin next year.

"Starlink Mobile is building the first fully-integrated US nationwide satellite and terrestrial network that will keep people connected on their phones no matter where they are," @Starlink posted at 8:32 a.m. EST, adding that its 5G base stations "will leverage @Qualcomm chips to accelerate deployment, starting in 2027."

SpaceX plans tens of thousands of new cell sites but has not named tower partners or said how many new towers it may need, according to CNBC. Starlink accounted for 36% of SpaceX's third-quarter revenue and 85% of its operating profit. Last month SpaceX agreed to buy a nationwide 800 MHz spectrum portfolio.

A challenger to the big three carriers

"Together with SpaceX, we are building world-leading cellular infrastructure in the United States, advancing 5G today, with an opportunity to lay the foundation for AI-native 6G in the years ahead," Qualcomm executive vice president Durga Malladi said, according to CNBC. Starlink Mobile is meant to compete with Verizon, AT&T and T-Mobile; SpaceX's satellite network alone cannot yet provide all the voice, video and data coverage those carriers offer on phones. Earlier this week the Washington Post reported that SpaceX is in talks to acquire EchoStar's Boost Mobile, CNBC noted. Qualcomm's earlier 5G infrastructure deals include Verizon, Dish and Vodafone.

Free Wi-Fi on every Air Tahiti Nui 787-9

Air Tahiti Nui said Tuesday it has signed an agreement with SpaceX to equip its entire Boeing 787-9 fleet with Starlink, offering free Wi-Fi in all travel classes with 25–30 millisecond latency from takeoff to landing. The rollout is expected in 2027. Passengers and crew will have Starlink "from gate to gate," @Starlink posted at 3:19 p.m. EST.

ZIPAIR credits Starlink for revenue growth

Japan Airlines' low-cost carrier ZIPAIR grew passenger revenue 12.3% year on year in the six months to Sept. 30, helped by "the completed installation of 'Starlink', high-speed internet service across all aircraft" and flexible pricing, the group said in its first-half results. "Starlink grows customers for airlines," Musk wrote at 11:10 a.m. EST Tuesday. Group revenue rose 11.7% to ¥1,098.9 billion.

IV · AI & Compute

"The world's biggest AI cloud"

SemiAnalysis counts 3.6GW under construction toward a 13GW goal; Texas may pause new data centers.

SpaceX has revised its estimate for data-center capacity in 2027 up to about 13GW from 10GW, and 3.6GW is already under construction across several sites, research firm SemiAnalysis wrote Tuesday in a report Musk reposted at 3:55 p.m. EST. The firm said it has identified more than 9GW of capacity and called the 13GW goal "roughly equivalent to all of Google's datacenter capacity from 1999-2026 in just 1.5 years."

The "Gun Range" site at Colossus 2 in Mississippi will take about four months to build, and the "Project SPAM" building, started Oct. 5, targets December for its first tranches, SemiAnalysis said. Starbase in Texas will house two data centers, and the firm has "medium-high confidence" that the TeraFab chip-fab project will have one too.

Battery storage of 5.9GWh is already being installed at Colossus 2, and Macrohard (700MW) and MiniHard (450MW) have requested grid service from TVA, the regional utility (SemiAnalysis).

Record build times, and a power squeeze

SpaceX's first retrofit at Colossus 1 took 122 days for 150MW, and Colossus 2 took 91 days for 210MW; Colossus 2 adds nearly two Stargate Abilenes (2GW) in just under one year, SemiAnalysis wrote. Power is tighter. A 500kV gas-insulated substation next to MiniHard is scheduled to finish by Dec. 31, 2026, while the turbines at the Southaven plant can legally run 2.7GW nameplate today but only 1.24GW by July 2027. The firm sees a possible gap of up to 300MW and expects the large battery field to help cover it.

Texas: a moratorium on the table

"Data centers in space looking pretty appealing right now," Musk posted at 1:12 a.m. EST Wednesday after the midterm results. Barclays analysts flagged implications for the AI buildout in Texas, where Governor-elect Gina Hinojosa campaigned on a moratorium on new data-center construction until the legislature sets more requirements, Investing.com reported. Her conditions include union labor, community input, water and power protections, and operators covering their own infrastructure costs.

SpaceX was down 3.8% in premarket trading, Reuters reported, and about 5% in a later Investing.com report. The company earns billions from government contracts, and greater congressional scrutiny could complicate its AI expansion, Investing.com wrote.
