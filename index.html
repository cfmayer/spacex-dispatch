The Morning Picture

Results tomorrow, and a share for every child

SpaceX reports its third quarter after the close on Tuesday, its second report as a public company, and up to 1.3 billion locked-up shares become sellable two trading days later. "Next year, I will give one of my SpaceX shares to every kid in America. This will be the first of many," Elon Musk posted at 3:52 a.m. EDT Monday, a gift worth about $10.5 billion at Friday's close, CNBC calculated.

Bandwagon-5 slipped a day to Tuesday because of recovery weather. New York City Council members called SpaceXAI's testimony on Grok safety evasive, and Ukraine's president blamed Washington for Starshield speed limits. SPCX fell 1.74% to $146.03 on Friday.

Tomorrow · Q3 earnings

Third-quarter results on Tuesday

Tue Oct 20 · results after the close · audio-only webcast 4:30 p.m. EDT

SpaceX set the date on Friday; the webcast will also stream on X (SpaceX). Analysts expect earnings of $0.17 a share on $13.02 billion in revenue, according to Yahoo Finance (17 and 18 estimates, retrieved Monday). Second-quarter revenue was $7.8 billion, with a $541 million net loss, TeslaNorth noted. Under the prospectus, up to 1.3 billion more Class A shares, 28% of those under the 180-day lock-up, are released on the second full trading day after the results: Thursday, October 22.

I · Launches & Starship

A weather day for Bandwagon-5

No SpaceX launches since Thursday night; Starship aims for a double catch in November.

Bandwagon-5

"Now targeting no earlier than Tuesday, October 20 for Falcon 9's launch of the Bandwagon-5 rideshare mission from Florida due to unfavorable recovery weather conditions," SpaceX posted at 3:27 a.m. EDT Monday. Teams had encapsulated 32 payloads on Saturday (SpaceX). The window opens at 6:30 a.m. EDT; booster B1085, on its 19th flight, is to land on the drone ship A Shortfall of Gravitas, Spaceflight Now lists.

Starship Flights 15 and 16

Flight 15 is targeting November 8, with the goal of catching both Booster 22 and Ship 42, but both still need static fires; Ship 42's engines were removed for an extra cryogenic test, NASASpaceflight reported on Sunday. Musk has said the flight should come before Thanksgiving. Flight 16, likely Booster 23 and Ship 43, is aimed at December and could be the first Starship launch from Launch Complex 39A in Florida.

III · Starlink

Kyiv and Washington spar over Starshield limits

Zelenskyy says the U.S. is behind the speed cap; the Treasury secretary told him to "be very careful."

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said on NBC's "Meet the Press" on Sunday that he believed the U.S. government was responsible for the slowdown and that Starshield-linked vehicles and drones are still restricted in speed, CNBC reported. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent answered that President Trump and Secretary of State Marco Rubio "have categorically said that no one under the Trump administration did that," and that "President Zelenskyy should be very careful about making statements like this that aren't true." Musk wrote last week that "any limits on speed or usage are decided by the US military and ultimately POTUS."

Quick Hit

Southwest. The airline has 47 aircraft connected to Starlink and expects its whole fleet equipped by fall 2027, its chief customer officer told AirlineGeeks. "Southwest Airlines going all-in with @Starlink!" Musk posted.

IV · SpaceXAI & Shareholders

"Simply unacceptable," the Council speaker says

A subpoenaed SpaceXAI manager testified on Grok safety; Musk answered Anthropic with a share giveaway.

Before the City Council

Gabriela Castillo Madrid, SpaceXAI's senior manager of global government affairs, told the Committee on Technology by video on Friday that the company has "strict controls," CNBC reported. Speaker Julie Menin said she had made a sexualized image of herself with Grok that morning: "how is it that within a second, I could find a sexualized image of myself using Grok?" Castillo Madrid said the safety team would look into it. "Their testimony was simply unacceptable," Menin said afterward (NYC Council). Former employee Devin Kim testified that only three of the company's 200 technical employees worked on AI safety training; Castillo Madrid said under oath she was not aware of his firing.

Quick Hit

Compute. Quoting a post titled "What it's like trying to rent AI/SI compute," Musk wrote that it "describes the conversation SpaceX has had when we have tried to rent inference compute from AWS, GCP or anyone at, so that we can shift load off our training clusters!!"

One share per child

Anthropic said Sunday it will commit 5% of its shares to a new public benefit fund, subject to stockholder approval (Anthropic). "I recommend giving the shares directly to the public," Musk replied, then "This is what I will be doing." He did not say how a gift to the roughly 72 million U.S. children would work; he owns about 4.8 billion SpaceX shares, CNBC noted.

V · Filings: SEC, FAA & FCC

No new filings before the results

No new SEC filings or FAA Federal Register notices since Friday's issue; the Starbase review remains open.

FAA

  • Notice of intent · Oct 14 — The FAA will prepare a Programmatic Environmental Impact Statement (PEIS) on SpaceX's proposal to increase Starship-Super Heavy operations at Starbase (docket FAA-2026-12808). Phase 1 would allow 100 launches a year at any hour; Phase 2 up to 456, and even Phase 1 is not expected for at least a year, NASASpaceflight noted. Comments are due November 30. Federal Register
